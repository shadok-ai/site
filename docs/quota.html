Quota and pace

Every agent consumes your Claude quota like a normal session. Five agents working is five sessions' worth of usage. That is the one cost model to keep in mind, and the cockpit puts it on screen rather than letting you find out at the end of the week.

The gauges

Both subscription windows, live: the 5-hour one and the 7-day one. The bar's centre is the pace that would spend the window exactly as it elapses, so the question it answers is not "how much is left" but "am I ahead of the clock".

Beside them, each agent carries a context bar — how full its conversation is, measured against the window the model actually asked for, so an agent on a long-context model is metered against that.

The pace guard

Turn it on and a prompt is refused when usage has run ahead of the ideal pace. The agent is not killed and nothing is lost: the prompt is held, you are told, and anything can force through when you mean it.

Two consequences worth knowing:

  • A scheduled fire blocked this way is treated as a transient miss and retried about ten minutes later, up to three times, never past its next normal slot. See Scheduled prompts.
  • A spawn blocked this way is silent to the parent agent. If you are building something on top of agents spawning agents, surface it.

The way out

An agent on a model you host yourself spends nothing of your subscription, so it is never held by the pace guard and needs no Claude sign-in. Pointing one role at a local model and leaving the rest on the subscription is a profile away.
